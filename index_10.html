    If you leave your job

    Company cover usually ends on your last working day. HR can confirm the exact date.

    • Last dayCompany cover stops for you and your family, including the you are counting on today.
    • Before you goYou can ask to move to the same insurer's individual policy. The insurer may check your health first, cover is usually capped at your current sum insured, and the premium can differ.
    • New policyA fresh personal policy has waiting periods: 30 days for illness (accidents are covered from day one), and up to 3 years for conditions you already have.

    Buying while you are still employed means those waiting years pass while your company cover still protects you.

    How the claim is worked out

    We assume 40% of the non-room bill is medicines, consumables, tests and implants, which room limits don't cut. Disease-wise caps, non-payable items and hospital packages are not included. Your real claim depends on your policy wording.

    Questions to ask HR
    1. What is my sum insured, and is it shared by my whole family?
    2. Is there a room rent limit? How much per day?
    3. Is there a co-pay when my parents are admitted?
    4. Are there caps on specific treatments, like cataract or maternity?
    5. On which date does my cover end if I resign?
    6. Can I move to an individual policy with the same insurer when I leave?
    7. Can I buy extra cover for my family through the company?
